# Women in India -- Narrative Outline

## Thesis
India's progress on gender equity is real in classrooms and on paper, stalled in workplaces and legislatures, and reversed on the streets. The data across six domains reveals a pattern of "progress that plateaus" -- breakthroughs in access that fail to translate into power or safety.

## Domains Used (and why)
- **Elections**: Women MPs trend (4.5% in 1957 to 13.6% in 2024). Political representation as the ultimate measure of institutional voice.
- **Employment**: Female LFPR (~28.8%) -- one of the lowest globally. Economic independence as the bridge between formal rights and lived reality.
- **Education**: Literacy gender gap narrowing, girls outnumbering boys in secondary enrollment in some states. The domain where progress is most visible.
- **Census**: Sex ratio, literacy rates, demographic structure. The baseline population facts.
- **Healthcare**: Maternal mortality, child marriage, NFHS-5 health indicators. The body-level consequences of inequality.
- **Crime**: 4.45 lakh registered crimes against women (2022). The safety dimension that undermines all other progress.

## Argument Arc

### Beat 1: Political Voice
Women's representation in Lok Sabha has crawled from 4.5% to 13.6% over seven decades. At this pace, parity is a century away. The 33% reservation bill (2023) promises change, but implementation depends on delimitation -- which has no timeline.
- Emotional register: Impatience.

### Beat 2: Economic Participation
India's female LFPR (~28.8%) is among the lowest in the world. Recent recovery is real but concentrated in agriculture and self-employment -- not formal, salaried work. Education gains are not converting into economic power.
- Emotional register: Frustration at the disconnect.

### Beat 3: Education and Health -- Where Progress Is Real
Literacy gender gap has shrunk from 25pp to ~13.5pp. Girls now outnumber boys in secondary enrollment in many states. Infant mortality has halved. But maternal mortality and child marriage remind us this progress is geographically uneven.
- Emotional register: Hope, qualified.

### Beat 4: Safety and Violence
4.45 lakh crimes against women registered in 2022 -- cruelty by husband, kidnapping, and assault leading. This is widely believed to be a fraction of actual incidence. Safety undermines every other gain: what does higher enrollment mean if girls drop out because the route to school is unsafe?
- Emotional register: Anger, urgency.

## Why This Matters
Gender equity is not a "women's issue" -- it is an economic and democratic one. India cannot claim its demographic dividend if half the population is underemployed. It cannot claim democratic health if women hold 13.6% of seats. The data across these six domains tells one story: India has opened doors but has not made the rooms behind them safe or accessible.
